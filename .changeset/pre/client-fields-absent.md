A client record no longer reads a field the answer did not carry as `false`, `0` or `""`. `uid`, `serverGroups`, `isAway`, `isRecording`, `isTalker` and `channelGroupID` are now optional on `ClientEntry` (and so on `ClientEnterEvent` and `view.clients`), and `createdAt`, `lastConnectedAt` and `idleTimeMs` are optional keys rather than keys holding `undefined`. `clientlist` carries only what its flags bring: `-uid` the uid, `-away` `isAway` and the away message, `-voice` `isRecording` and `isTalker` with the mute and hardware flags, `-groups` `serverGroups` and `channelGroupID`, `-times` the three times. So `listClients(client, { uid: false })` used to give every client a uid of `""` and a `serverGroups` of `[]`; it now gives neither. A client that came into view through `notifycliententerview` carries all of them. `nickname`, `channelID`, `type` and `databaseID` are required: every list and every enter notification sends them (`databaseID` was optional and is now required, so a record built by hand needs it). Migrate by asking for the flag you read (`listClients(client, { groups: true })`) and handling `undefined`: `entry.isAway` is `true`, `false` or `undefined` for "not asked", where `if (entry.isAway === false)` once matched every client.
