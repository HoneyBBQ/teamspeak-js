`PermissionName` names the `i_needed_modify_power_*` permissions, one per permission and called `NeededModifyPower` followed by that permission's name, such as `PermissionName.NeededModifyPowerVirtualServerCanonicalNameManage`. The seven TeamSpeak 6 permissions that had no name for their twin are covered with the rest.
