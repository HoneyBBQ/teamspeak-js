Client: a `connect()` that the caller's signal ends says what it was waiting for. `connect()` still rejects with the signal's own reason, as before, so nothing that matches it changes. The `attemptFailed` trace hook gets `waitingFor`, such as `the server's answer to clientek`, finer than `phase`, and the client logs the same when an attempt ends. A TeamSpeak 6 server sometimes never answers `clientek`, and the attempt then ended on the caller's deadline with no sign of where it stopped.
