The `@honeybbq/teamspeak/stream` entry point is no longer `@beta`: its exports follow the package's semver like the rest. TeamSpeak does not document the stream protocol; it was verified against the TeamSpeak 6 server 6.0.0-beta13.1 and the TeamSpeak 6 client 6.0.0-beta5, and a TeamSpeak update may break it.
